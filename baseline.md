The summariser reads this every morning alongside `profile.md`. Its job is to
stop settled facts being reported back as if they were news: once a reshuffle
has happened, a podcast explaining it is a recap, not a signal.

**Keep this current.** It is the one file in the repo that goes stale on its own.
Re-check it after any reshuffle, Budget, or major machinery-of-government change.

## Settled — established fact, NOT news

- Keir Starmer resigned as Prime Minister; **Andy Burnham succeeded him** (July
  2026), having returned to Parliament via the Makerfield by-election.
- **Rachel Reeves is no longer Chancellor.** John Healey replaced her.
- **Ed Miliband has left DESNZ** for the Foreign Office; Miatta Fahnbulleh
  replaced him as Energy Secretary.
- The department formerly "Business and Trade" is now **Business, Innovation,
  Science and Trade**.
- Burnham has committed to the 2024 manifesto's tax pledges (no rises to income
  tax rates, National Insurance or VAT) and to operating within existing fiscal
  rules.

A podcast or article that reports, explains or reacts to any of the above is
telling the reader something he already knows. It is **not** a signal.

## Genuinely open — still worth flagging

These are unsettled, so developments here ARE news:

- What Burnham's government actually does on energy: whether the Clean Power
  2030 mission, the SSEP, connections reform and allocation-round design survive
  the change of Secretary of State intact, and at what pace.
- Fahnbulleh's own priorities and whether DESNZ tilts from infrastructure
  delivery toward household bills and cost-of-living measures.
- The autumn Budget: how Healey funds the gap, and whether the manifesto tax
  pledges hold under pressure (defence spending is the named strain).
- Any further reshuffle, resignation or machinery-of-government change.
- Devolution and the Treasury's shape — repeatedly trailed, not yet enacted.
